# Business Requirements

Layer 1 (top). Parent of the product requirements. Solution-neutral: say what the business needs and how success is measured, not how it is built.
One requirement per `##` section. IDs are BR-001, BR-002 and so on, and are never reused.
Measurable outcomes are defined in `kpi-definition.md`. Status is draft, approved or deprecated.

## BR-001 <short title>
- **Statement:** The <business or organisation> shall ...
- **Rationale:** <why this matters>
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - AC1: <measurable condition>
- **KPI:** KPI-001
- **Children:** PRD-001
- **Verification level:** KPI definition
- **Status:** draft
