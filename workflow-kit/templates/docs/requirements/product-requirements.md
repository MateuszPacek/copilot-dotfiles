# Product Requirements

Layer 2. Parent: business requirements. Describes what the product does for its users, without prescribing the implementation.
One requirement per `##` section. IDs are PRD-001, PRD-002 and so on, and are never reused. Status is draft, approved or deprecated.

## PRD-001 <short title>
- **Statement:** The product shall ...
- **Parent:** BR-001
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - AC1: Given <context>, when <action>, then <observable result>
- **Children:** TR-001
- **Verification level:** Functional test (tag tests with PRD-001)
- **Status:** draft
